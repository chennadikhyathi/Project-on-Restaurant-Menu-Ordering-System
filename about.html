About US...

 The Seven Stars Inn is the original Chester County steakhouse, featuring overly generous portions of the finest beef, veal and seafood served in a gracious white table cloth atmosphere. The cocktail lounge is warm and inviting, and the well thought out wine list includes a broad spectrum of varietals and geographic origins. Family friendly, the Seven Stars has not only a great Children’s Menu but also a Young Adult’s Menu. Both of these menus scale down the size of the serving and price of their offerings to help mom and dad afford an evening out with the whole family.
